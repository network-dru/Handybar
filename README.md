<h1 align="center">Handybar</h1>

<p align="center">
  A quick-access toolkit for Windows: a small pill at the edge of your screen that opens into your everyday tools.
</p>

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9PMV3X81XH7W?hl=en-gb&gl=US&ocid=pdpshare"><img src="https://get.microsoft.com/images/en-us%20dark.svg" width="320" alt="Get it from Microsoft" /></a>
</p>

<p align="center">
  Everything runs on your PC. Handybar doesn't collect or send any data, and never connects to the internet.
</p>
