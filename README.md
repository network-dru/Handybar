# Handybar

A quick-access toolkit for Windows: a small pill at the edge of your screen that opens into your everyday tools.

- **Quick launch**: your favourite apps, one click away
- **Clipboard**: a history of what you've copied, with filters, plus copying text from anything on screen
- **Emoji**: search and type any emoji into the app you're using
- **Timer and stopwatch**
- **Now playing**: see and control what's playing
- **Shelf**: park files and drag them out later
- **Privacy lights**: see when an app is using your camera or microphone
- **Quick toggles**: mute your microphone or sound, switch light and dark mode

Everything runs on your PC. Handybar doesn't collect or send any data, and never connects to the internet.
